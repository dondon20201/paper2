## PART B — Recovery & separability (Step 0b) — FAILED

Everything in Part B is **SIMULATION** — planted-truth data generated by
`R/simulate.R`; no empirical result. The only real-data touch (declared):
the per-domain empirical accuracy range 0.70–0.77 from step 0a was read to
calibrate the simulator's d′ target (mean d′ 1.2 → accuracy ≈ 0.73).

**Generator.** Each simulated subject has three independent planted traits:
type-1 d′ (accuracy), metacognitive sensitivity λ (how strongly confidence
evidence tracks decision evidence |x|), and level/set-point β (shifts
confidence on the scale). Confidence evidence is analytically standardized
(folded-normal mean/sd given the subject's d′) so E = β and Var = 1 for every
(d′, λ) — the knobs are mechanically separate by construction; the only
possible coupling channel is the fixed 1–7 binning. Cohorts: 322 subjects
(matching Lund 2025), 2000-resample bootstrap CIs, fixed seeds
(20260710 primary; 42424242 spot-check).

**Estimator unit checks — PASS (VERIFIED, simulation).** `auroc2()` and
`gk_gamma()` match brute-force pair counting to < 1e-12 on 20 random datasets
(this check runs inside the script on every execution).

**Simulator self-check — PASS with quantified couplings (VERIFIED, simulation).**
200,000-trial cells:

- Varying planted level β (−1.5…+1.5): mean confidence strictly monotone ✓;
  accuracy flat (range 0.0036) ✓; AUROC2 nearly flat — residual coupling
  range **0.029** via binning compression at scale ends (quantified, small).
- Varying planted sensitivity λ (0.1…0.9): AUROC2 strictly monotone ✓;
  accuracy flat (range 0.0038) ✓; mean confidence range **0.16** — a modest
  binning-skew coupling (higher λ makes the pre-binning evidence more skewed).
  Note this did NOT translate into level-estimate leakage (below: r = −0.02).
- Generated accuracy matches its analytic target: max |diff| 0.003 ✓.

### B1. Recovery of planted traits (preregistered thresholds)

| Measure | 200 trials r [95% CI] | 100 trials r [95% CI] | Criterion | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level (mean conf → β) | **0.99** [0.99, 0.99] | **0.98** [0.98, 0.99] | ≥ 0.9 both | **PASS** |
| Sensitivity (AUROC2 → λ) | **0.59** [0.52, 0.65] | 0.45 [0.35, 0.53] | ≥ 0.7 at 200 | **FAIL** |
| Sensitivity (gamma → λ) | **0.55** [0.48, 0.62] | 0.44 [0.34, 0.52] | ≥ 0.7 at 200 | **FAIL** |

**Why sensitivity recovery fails — diagnosed, not a bug (VERIFIED, simulation):**

- *Not an implementation error:* estimators match brute force exactly, and at
  5000 trials/subject recovery rises to **0.82** [0.78, 0.85] (AUROC2) — the
  estimator is consistent; 100–200 trials is simply not enough signal.
- *Split-half reliability* of AUROC2 at 200 trials is only ≈ 0.42–0.52
  (≈ 0.25–0.31 at 100 trials), which caps recovery near √rel ≈ 0.65–0.72
  before any other problem.
- *Type-1 confound:* AUROC2 and gamma correlate ≈ **0.33** [0.23, 0.41] with
  planted d′ at 200 trials (0.41–0.43 at 5000) — a known structural limitation
  of type-2 measures, now quantified; it steals recovery from λ even
  asymptotically.

**Criterion-calibration note (dispatch invites this; verdict unchanged):** the
≥ 0.7 threshold at 200 trials was likely never achievable for AUROC2 given a
realistic trait spread — the reliability ceiling alone sits at ≈ 0.65–0.72.
This is a fact about the measure at these trial counts, i.e. exactly the
"sensitivity is poorly measured" warning in BRIEF.md, now with numbers.

### B2. Separability / leakage (at 200 trials; preregistered |r| < 0.10, CI ∋ 0)

| Direction | r [95% CI] | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Estimated level ← planted sensitivity | −0.020 [−0.13, 0.091] | **PASS** |
| Estimated AUROC2 ← planted level | −0.035 [−0.15, 0.077] | **PASS** |
| Estimated gamma ← planted level | **−0.18** [−0.32, −0.025] | **FAIL** |

Mean confidence and AUROC2 are cleanly separable — the decisive check the
BRIEF demanded. **Gamma, however, leaks planted level** (replicates on the
second seed: −0.15): extreme set-points concentrate confidence in few
categories, and gamma's tie-exclusion turns that into bias. **Recommendation:
drop gamma as a primary sensitivity measure; keep AUROC2.**

### B3. Thesis-detectability — the decisive failure

Two domains, planted cross-domain correlation of level vs sensitivity;
"truth" = the realized correlation among the drawn traits.

**Gap scenario (ρ_level 0.6, ρ_sens 0.2) — the design detects the direction
but badly attenuates sensitivity:** estimated level correlation ≈ truth in
every config (e.g. 200/200: est 0.51 [0.43, 0.59], truth 0.53 ✓), while the
estimated sensitivity correlation collapses (200/200: est **0.084**
[−0.02, 0.19] vs truth 0.32 — truth OUTSIDE the CI). Level > sensitivity holds
everywhere, but sensitivity's absolute value is not recovered → criterion
"each estimate within its CI of truth" **FAIL**.

**Null scenario (both ρ = 0.4) — a spurious raw gap appears:** estimated raw
gap 0.27–0.31 with bootstrap CIs excluding zero in ALL three trial configs
(e.g. 200/200: 0.31 [0.17, 0.44]) despite zero planted gap. **A naive
raw-correlation contrast on the real data would therefore manufacture the
thesis out of reliability differences alone.** Split-half disattenuation
shrinks the null gap (to 0.08–0.20, seed 1) but is itself unstable across
seeds (seed 2: −0.11 to +0.25) — Spearman-Brown correction with these
reliabilities is too noisy at n = 322 to certify a zero. Criterion **FAIL**.

### B4. Seed stability

All conclusions replicate qualitatively on the second seed (recovery within
±0.06; gamma leakage −0.18 vs −0.15; spurious null raw gaps 0.16–0.35 across
both seeds). The unstable quantity is the disattenuated gap, as noted.

## WHAT FAILING MEANS, AND OPTIONS

Per BRIEF.md, Step 1+ (any thesis analysis on real data) does **not** proceed
until a revised Step 0 passes. Nothing here was silently relaxed; the
preregistered criteria stand and were failed.

What is now on solid ground:
1. The data is real, verified, and understood (0a).
2. Confidence LEVEL is measured essentially without error and without leakage
   — any future level-generalization claim rests on a validated measure.
3. The estimators are provably correct code; their limits are quantified.

What is broken, precisely:
4. AUROC2/gamma at 100–200 trials are too unreliable to support the planned
   raw Step-3 contrast, and gamma is additionally biased by set-point.
5. Raw correlation comparison invents the thesis under a null; simple
   disattenuation does not stably fix it.

Options for Cowork to weigh (my recommendation is (a)):
- **(a) Redesign the thesis test around a latent/structural estimator** — the
  Step-4 joint multilevel model brought INTO the gate: extend this simulation
  to test whether a hierarchical latent-correlation model recovers ρ_sens and
  the level-vs-sensitivity gap without bias at these trial counts; proceed
  only if it passes the same planted-truth discipline. (This also handles the
  ipsative artifact by design.)
- **(b) Reframe the paper's primary claim to what the validated machinery can
  support** — level generalization (bulletproof) + honest measurement-limits
  finding for sensitivity (the BRIEF's "still useful if the exciting part
  fails" floor, with the spurious-gap demonstration as a methodological
  contribution: raw comparisons of this kind in the literature are suspect).
- **(c) Both.** (a) determines whether the strong claim is testable; (b) is
  the floor either way.
